- Microsoft's announcement says Into the Abyss, live on August 11, 2026, is Grounded 2's largest content update yet, adding an expansive underwater adventure beneath Skunk Pond in Brookhollow Park.
- The update introduces diving gear, underwater base building, new creatures including the amphibious Toe Biter and mechanical Catch 'N Kill robots, plus the amphibious Toe Biter Buggy.
- Grounded 2 is launching on PlayStation 5 during Early Access, joining Xbox Series X|S, Xbox on PC, Steam, Game Pass Ultimate, and PC Game Pass.
