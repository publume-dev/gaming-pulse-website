- Compulsion Games is now independent from Xbox, with management acquiring both the studio and its staff.
- The studio has regained ownership of its IP, including Contrast, We Happy Few, and South of Midnight, and publishing rights are transferring back.
- CEO Guillaume Provost stated the studio is exploring partnerships and funding alternatives to become self-sustaining, and is open to collaborating with other developers and IP holders.
