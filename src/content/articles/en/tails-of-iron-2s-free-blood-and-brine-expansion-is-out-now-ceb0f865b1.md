- The expansion is free and available for PS5, PS4, Xbox Series X|S, and Xbox One.
- It adds a new coastal region, Hardshell Brook, with 11 quests, 10 new bosses, nine armor sets, new music, and a fishing system featuring 32 aquatic creatures.
- Players must complete the main campaign before accessing the expansion.
- The expansion includes crossover armor inspired by Blasphemous, Cult of the Lamb, Dave the Diver, Death's Door, Moonlighter, and Beyond Galaxyland.
- New ship arena battles are introduced for combat on the open water.
