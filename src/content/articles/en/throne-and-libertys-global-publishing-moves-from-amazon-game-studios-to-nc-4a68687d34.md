- **Change**: Throne and Liberty's global publishing service will transfer from Amazon Game Studios to NC, aiming to complete the migration by Q4 2026.
- **Scope**: Affects players on all platforms, including Steam, PlayStation, Xbox, and NC's Purple service.
- **Unchanged**: Players' account details will remain the same across all platforms.
