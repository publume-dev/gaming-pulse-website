- Nexus Mods is acquiring SteamDB, a database and analytics site that tracks Steam games.
- SteamDB founder xPaw stated the goal is to give the project resources, development capacity, and long-term stability.
- Nexus Mods co-owner Victor Folmann said the acquisition will help make modding easier by connecting SteamDB's build and update data with mod metadata.
