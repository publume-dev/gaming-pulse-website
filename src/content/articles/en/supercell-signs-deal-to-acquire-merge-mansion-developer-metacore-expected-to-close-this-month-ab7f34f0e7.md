- Supercell has signed a deal to acquire Merge Mansion developer Metacore, with the acquisition expected to close at the end of September.
- Once closed, Merge Mansion will become part of Supercell's live games portfolio, and both companies are already planning the transition.
- The report notes that Metacore previously considered reducing up to 160 roles in Finland and investigating changes in Germany and Sweden, but these plans are not mentioned in the latest announcement, leaving their status unclear.
