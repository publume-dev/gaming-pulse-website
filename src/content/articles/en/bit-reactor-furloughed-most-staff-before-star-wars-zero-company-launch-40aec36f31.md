- Bit Reactor confirmed it furloughed a number of workers before Star Wars Zero Company's launch on August 27, 2026, due to concerns about how the title would perform.
- Sources claim approximately 80 percent of Bit Reactor's staff were furloughed, leaving a skeleton crew to address post-launch bugs.
- Bit Reactor's representative said the studio hopes to reinstate furloughed employees and restore their pay if Star Wars Zero Company proves successful.
- Bit Reactor ran out of money to pay employees shortly before the launch, despite hopes of securing another game deal.
- The decision was not influenced by publisher Electronic Arts or Star Wars license holder Disney, according to a Bit Reactor representative.
