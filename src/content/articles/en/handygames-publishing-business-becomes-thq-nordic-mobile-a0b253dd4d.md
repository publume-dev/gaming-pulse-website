- Publishing operations now run under the THQ Nordic Mobile branding, consolidating them under the THQ Nordic umbrella.
- THQ Nordic Mobile will focus on bringing premium PC and console titles to mobile platforms.
- The internal development studio in Giebelstadt keeps operating under the HandyGames name.
- Co-founders Christopher Kassulke and Markus Kassulke remain in charge of both the publishing label and the development studio.
- Existing development pipelines, live-title support, and licensing agreements continue unaffected.
