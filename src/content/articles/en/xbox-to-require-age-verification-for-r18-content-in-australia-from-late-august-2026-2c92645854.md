- Rollout begins in late August 2026; adult accounts must verify age before playing R18+ games or using R18+ apps on console and PC through the Microsoft Store and Xbox Store.
- The requirement is part of Microsoft's compliance program in Australia; R18+ apps and games users already own will not be affected by access restrictions.
- VerifyMy will manage age verification, with email, facial age estimation, credit card, or government-issued ID as available methods.
