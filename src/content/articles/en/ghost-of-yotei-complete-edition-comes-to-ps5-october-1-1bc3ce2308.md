- Ghost of Yōtei Complete Edition launches on PS5 on October 1, 2026, including the main game, Legends, new expansion 'Echoes of Sekigahara', and new survival mode 'Most Wanted'.
- The Complete Edition is priced at $69.99 USD, with an upgrade path for existing owners at $14.99 USD.
- A free patch on October 1 adds the 'Beauty of Yōtei' mode, armor appearance options, and a high contrast accessibility option for all owners.
- Pre-ordering by September 30 grants early unlock of the Sakai Armor on October 1.
