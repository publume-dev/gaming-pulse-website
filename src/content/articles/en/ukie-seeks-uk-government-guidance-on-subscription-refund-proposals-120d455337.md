- Ukie has asked the UK government to clarify how the new consumer subscription refund proposals will affect video game companies operating in the UK.
- The government's proposal introduces a 14-day cooling-off period that would let consumers cancel subscription contracts without extra charges after a trial or long-term contract renews.
- Ukie is concerned that a narrow refund calculation method could let players consume content worth more than the refund before cancelling, potentially discouraging subscription investment and creating financial exposure for businesses.
- Ukie has requested that the government publish guidance for affected businesses to adapt their processes.
