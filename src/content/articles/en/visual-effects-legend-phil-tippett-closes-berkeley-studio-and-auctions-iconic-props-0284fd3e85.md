- Phil Tippett announced the closure of his Berkeley studio and his retirement, with his work sold at a two-day auction due to Tippett Studio's Chapter 11 bankruptcy.
- Tippett Studio clarified that while the Berkeley location is closed, it continues to operate in Canada and is not out of business, with its Toronto studio in active production on a large feature film.
