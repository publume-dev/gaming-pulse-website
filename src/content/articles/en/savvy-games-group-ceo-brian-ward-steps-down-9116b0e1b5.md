- Brian Ward is stepping down as CEO of Savvy Games Group.
- Turqi Alnowaiser, deputy governor of Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund and head of its International Investments Division, will serve as interim acting CEO.
- Ward had led Savvy since its inception in 2021, overseeing a $37.8 billion investment in the games industry.
- Under Ward, Savvy acquired Scopely for $4.9 billion in 2023 and Moonton for $6 million earlier this year.
- Bloomberg reports internal concern at Savvy over the PIF-led acquisition of EA for $55 billion, though the reason for Ward's exit is not specified.
