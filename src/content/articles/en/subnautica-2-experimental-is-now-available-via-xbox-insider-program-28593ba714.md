- Subnautica 2 Experimental is now available through the Xbox Insider Program on Xbox Series X|S and Windows PC, requiring ownership of Subnautica 2 or an active Game Pass subscription.
- Experimental builds contain in-development content and may be less stable, and multiplayer is restricted to other Experimental players.
- Experimental and Stable saves are kept separate and cannot be used interchangeably, but save data is not deleted.
