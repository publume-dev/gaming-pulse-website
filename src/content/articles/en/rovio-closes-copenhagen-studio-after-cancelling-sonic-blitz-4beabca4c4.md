- Rovio has closed its Copenhagen studio after cancelling its mobile title Sonic Blitz.
- Approximately 20 employees were working on Sonic Blitz, but the exact number of staff affected cannot be confirmed as the redundancy process is ongoing.
- Rovio CEO Alexandre Pelletier-Normand said the decision was needed to concentrate bets on games and locations, and that Sonic Blitz did not meet retention expectations.
- Sonic Blitz soft launched in 2025 in the Philippines and Brazil, but was shut down last month as part of a broader change at Rovio.
- Rovio will provide a comprehensive transition package for affected team members, including outplacement services and wellbeing support.
