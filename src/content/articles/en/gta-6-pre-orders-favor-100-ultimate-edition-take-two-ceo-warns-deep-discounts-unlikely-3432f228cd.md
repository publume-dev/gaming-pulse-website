- In an interview, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said GTA 6 pre-orders are currently tilting more toward the $100 Ultimate Edition than the $80 standard edition, partly because the most dedicated fans are reserving now.
- He said Take-Two kept Grand Theft Auto 5's pricing for a very long time. Retailers can set their own prices, but Zelnick said players should not expect deep discounts on GTA 6 anytime soon.
- Zelnick said a true physical release after launch is not completely off the table, and he doesn't think skipping a disc at launch will hurt sales.
