- Spanish union CSV is calling for three days of strike action every two weeks at Ubisoft Mobile Barcelona starting in September 2026.
- Strikes are scheduled for September 1, 2, 3, 16, 17, and 18.
- The union states Ubisoft has refused to negotiate with worker representatives and delayed the return-to-office from October to January 2027 without communication.
