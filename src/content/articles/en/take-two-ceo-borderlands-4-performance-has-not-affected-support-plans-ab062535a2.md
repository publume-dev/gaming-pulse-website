- In an IGN interview, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said Borderlands 4's commercial performance has not affected long-term support plans, stating "performance has not affected our plans at all" and "we're totally supporting it."
- Gearbox's Borderlands 4 Story Pack 2, adding Vault Hunter Loveless the Hacker, and Bounty Pack 5 arrive with Version 1.10 in early September.
- Borderlands 4 currently has a "mixed" user rating on Steam, with concurrent player peaks down from over 300,000 at launch to four digits.
- Gearbox has not announced a Year 2 roadmap; exact contents, pricing, and release timing remain unconfirmed.
