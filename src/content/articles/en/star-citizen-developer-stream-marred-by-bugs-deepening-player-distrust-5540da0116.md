- During a recent development stream, developers repeatedly encountered major bugs, including issues with weapon use, swapping, and inventory, and eventually ended the stream due to mounting frustration.
- One developer closed the stream saying they were proud of the team's work and wanted players to have the 'same experience' they had on stream, which fans saw as tone-deaf.
- Star Citizen has been in development for roughly 15 years and has raised over $1 billion from fans, and the game has been in early access since 2017 with no release date.

- The public display of broken core systems on stream directly contrasts with developer assurances, potentially further undermining player confidence in the project's progress.

- Watch whether Squadron 42 will meet its 2026 target and whether CIG formally addresses the stream incident.
