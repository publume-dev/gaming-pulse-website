- Release date: September 15 on PS5.
- The opening mission is set in Telambang and involves infiltrating Bolivar Trask's facility with Team X.
- Combat features special techniques like Tornado Spin and Knee Breaker, and a rage meter that rises through three tiers, unlocking Last Stand at tier 2 and critical strikes at tier 3.
- Exploration includes Princess Bar in Madripoor, easter eggs, collectibles, and Nightmare Doors that lead to timed speed trials.
