- Unity reported Q2 2026 total revenue of $546 million, a 24% increase year-over-year from $440 million.
- Net loss narrowed to $23 million from $108 million in Q2 2025.
- Grow division revenue rose 69% year-over-year, driven by developer spending on Vector AI.
- Create division revenue rose 2% year-over-year.
- CEO Matthew Bromberg described Q2 2026 as arguably the company's "best quarter ever."
- Unity's stock rose 20% from $35 to $42 per share after the earnings release.
- Unity announced plans to roll out Unity 7, which will include a "collaborative ecosystem" feature for rapid game updates.
