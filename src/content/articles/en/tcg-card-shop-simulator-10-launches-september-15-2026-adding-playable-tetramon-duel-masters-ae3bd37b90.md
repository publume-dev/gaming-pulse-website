- The 1.0 update launches September 15, 2026, adding the playable TCG Tetramon Duel Masters.
- Tetramon Duel Masters features 50-card decks, a five-card opening hand, one mulligan, 500 Tamer Points, and a comeback mechanic that draws from the Guardian Area at intervals of 400, 300, 200, and 100 points.
- The 1.0 update also introduces a fresh wave of new Tetramon cards to collect and use.
