- CD Projekt confirmed that four staff in Boston and five in Warsaw were laid off from Project Sirius, with nine others invited to apply for internal roles on other projects.
- Project Sirius had approximately 83 staff as of April 30, 2026, now closer to 60, a drop of about 28%.

The layoffs will affect the project's development and team morale, but the exact impact remains to be seen.
