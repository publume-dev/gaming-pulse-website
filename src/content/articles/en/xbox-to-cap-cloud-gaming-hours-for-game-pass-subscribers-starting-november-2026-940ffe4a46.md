- From November 2026, Game Pass Ultimate subscribers get 15 hours, Premium 10 hours, and Essential 5 hours of cloud gaming per month.
- Subscribers can buy extra cloud playtime via the Xbox Store after using their included hours.
- Starting November 2026, players without a Game Pass subscription will be able to buy cloud playtime to stream eligible owned games on supported devices.
- Microsoft expects these changes to affect 4% of Game Pass subscribers.
- The company cites rising cloud gaming costs and a need to keep investing in reliability and performance.
