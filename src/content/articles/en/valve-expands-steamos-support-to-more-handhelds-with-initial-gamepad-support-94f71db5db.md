- According to IGN, Valve's latest SteamOS update improves support for MSI Claw handhelds and adds initial gamepad support for the MSI Claw 8 EX AI+, Ayaneo Pocket S2, and Konkr Fit.
- IGN notes Valve previously said it was working on expanding SteamOS support beyond AMD hardware, with the update serving as the first concrete sign of that work.
- Whether these handhelds will receive full SteamOS support is unclear, since the update only mentions initial gamepad support for some devices.
