- Riot Games will end active development of 2XKO in December, with servers staying online beyond 2026 and offline play unaffected.
- Riot stated that 2XKO costs substantially more to operate than it brings in, and engagement has remained relatively flat despite larger updates.
- Purchases of KO Points and 2XKO Starter Edition on PC will be refunded to the original payment method, with most refunds completed within two weeks and the rest by November 2026.
- The September 1.3.1 patch will unlock all champion characters and most gameplay content, and a bundle with most cosmetics will be available for $39.99/£33.99/€39.99.
- The October 1.3.3 patch will introduce final champion Samira, and patch 1.3.5 in December will focus on bug fixes with no new content.
