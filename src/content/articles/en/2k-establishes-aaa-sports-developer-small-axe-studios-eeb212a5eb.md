- 2K has launched Small Axe Studios, an AAA development team based in Vancouver, Canada, led by Aaron McHardy.
- Aaron McHardy previously led EA Sports FIFA for 15 years until 2021.
- Small Axe Studios is developing 2K's next sports franchise and is recruiting for key positions.
- The studio originated from 2K Sports Lab, established in 2023, which focused on R&D for future sports interactive entertainment games.
