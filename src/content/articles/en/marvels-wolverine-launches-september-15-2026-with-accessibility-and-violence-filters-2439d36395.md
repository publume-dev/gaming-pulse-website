- Marvel's Wolverine releases on September 15, 2026 for PlayStation 5. 
- Includes over 100 new, returning, and improved accessibility features from previous Insomniac games, such as High Contrast visuals, Audio Description, Global Game Speed, and Strong Haptic Signals.
- New categories for violence and adult language toggles allow altering dynamic blood, dismemberment, and filtering English-only adult language.
- At launch, New Game Plus, Mission Replay, Nightmare Challenges, and Photo Mode are available via a day one update.
