- According to IGN, the median price of the Nvidia GeForce RTX 5060 Ti 16GB has risen to $804.99, up 39% from $569 in June 2026.
- The median price of the RTX 5070 has risen to $899, up 36% from $659 in June 2026.
- Those median prices are 61% above the RTX 5060 Ti's $429 MSRP and 57% above the RTX 5070's $499 MSRP.
- Newegg currently lists the cheapest RTX 5060 Ti 16GB at $779 and a low-profile RTX 5070 at $749.
