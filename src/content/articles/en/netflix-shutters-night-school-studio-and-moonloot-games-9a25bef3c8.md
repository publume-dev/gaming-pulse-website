- Netflix has closed Night School Studio and Helsinki-based Moonloot Games.
- Netflix is also eliminating additional roles across its games teams; the number of affected employees was not disclosed.
- Night School Studio released horror game Unhinged in late June 2026, just weeks before the closure.
- Netflix acquired Night School Studio in 2021; it was the first studio acquired by Netflix's games business.
- Moonloot was founded in 2022 and had no announced projects.
- Next Games is now the only internal studio at Netflix, in addition to Netflix's gaming team.
- Netflix previously shut down Team Blue in 2024 and Boss Fight in October 2025, and sold Spry Fox back to its founders in December 2025.
