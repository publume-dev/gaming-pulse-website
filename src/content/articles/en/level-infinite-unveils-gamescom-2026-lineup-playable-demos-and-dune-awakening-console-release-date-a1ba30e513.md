- Level Infinite will feature playable demos at Gamescom 2026 of Control Resonant (first hour), Dune: Awakening (console version), and Hunt: Showdown 1896.
- Dune: Awakening's console version releases September 22 and includes a new single-player solo mode.
- An exclusive Hunt: Showdown 1896 reveal will air during IGN's Gamescom coverage on Wednesday, August 26.

The Hunt: Showdown 1896 reveal has been described as 'fresh, unique' and involving 'major rebalancing', but specifics are not yet disclosed.
