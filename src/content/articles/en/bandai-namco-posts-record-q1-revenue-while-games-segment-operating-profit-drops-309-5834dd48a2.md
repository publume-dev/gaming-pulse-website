- Group Q1 net sales rose 9.3% to ¥328.4 billion, operating profit rose 33.3% to ¥69.2 billion, and ordinary profit rose 36.3% to ¥74.4 billion, all record results.
- The Digital segment (games) saw Q1 net sales fall 15.6% to ¥90.9 billion and operating profit fall 30.9% to ¥15 billion.
- The Home Console Game segment sold 6.9 million units with two new titles in the quarter, versus 10.8 million units and 15 new titles a year earlier.
- The half-year Digital Contents forecast was revised to sales up 10% at ¥200 billion and operating profit up 11.5% at ¥26.5 billion, though both remain down year-on-year.
- Announced releases: Ace Combat 8: Wings of Theve on October 2, 2026; Dragon Ball Xenoverse 3 and Gundam Rogue Orbit in 2027.

- Bandai Namco kept its full-year forecast unchanged and said it will reassess based on market changes, fan needs, major home console titles in the third quarter, and key selling seasons.
