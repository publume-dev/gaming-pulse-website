- Led by Myles Kerwin, former Ubisoft Barcelona employees founded Dark Ritual Studios after Ubisoft eliminated 51 positions at the Barcelona studio.
- First game Puzzle Mage is a dark fantasy title targeting a 2027 PC release.
- Puzzle Mage has surpassed 1,000 wishlists on Steam.
- Dark Ritual Studios has secured funding to complete its first game and is weighing self-publishing against a publisher partnership.
