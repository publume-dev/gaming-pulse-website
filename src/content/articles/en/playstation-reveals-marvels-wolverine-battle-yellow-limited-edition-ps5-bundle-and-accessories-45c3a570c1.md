- **PS5 Digital Edition – Marvel’s Wolverine Battle Yellow Limited Edition Bundle** includes a matching DualSense controller and a game code for *Marvel’s Wolverine*; pre-orders begin August 19, 2026 at 10 AM local time.
- Bundle RRP is $649.99 US / £569.99 GBP / €649.99 EUR / ¥96,980 JPY. The standalone Battle Yellow DualSense controller costs $84.99 US / £74.99 GBP / €84.99 EUR / ¥12,480 JPY.
- Additional accessories include Battle Yellow PS5 and PS5 Pro console covers, an Adamantium-themed PS5 Pro cover set, and an Adamantium controller. Covers are priced at $74.99 US / £64.99 GBP / €74.99 EUR / ¥9,980 JPY; the Adamantium controller costs $84.99 US / £74.99 GBP / €84.99 EUR.
- All Marvel’s Wolverine Limited Edition products arrive September 15, 2026 in limited quantities, the same date as the game’s launch.
