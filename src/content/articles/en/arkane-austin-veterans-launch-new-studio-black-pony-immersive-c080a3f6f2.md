- **New studio**: Founded by Arkane Austin veterans Harvey Smith (creative director on Dishonored 2) and Ben Horne (production director on Redfall), named Black Pony Immersive.
- **Timeline**: Opened in August 2025 and publicly announced on August 19, 2026, nearly two years after Microsoft shut down Arkane Austin in 2024.
- **Team**: 26 employees, with talent from Arkane Austin, Insomniac, Avalanche, Volition, and Epic; all full-time staff are based in the United States.
- **Current project**: Developing an unannounced single-player, narrative-rich game leveraging the team's expertise in emergent game mechanics.
