- State of Play and State of Play Japan will air back-to-back on September 3, 2026, starting at 6:00am PT / 9:00am ET / 3:00pm CEST / 10:00pm JST on YouTube and Twitch.
- The main State of Play will include updates and announcements from PlayStation Studios and third-party partners, and conclude with an extended look at Final Fantasy VII Revelation from Square Enix.
- State of Play Japan, hosted by Yuki Kaji, will offer deep dives into upcoming games from studios in Japan and Asia, including new gameplay and announcements.
