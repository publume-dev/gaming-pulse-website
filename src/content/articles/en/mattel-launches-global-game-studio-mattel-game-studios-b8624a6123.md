- Mattel has formed Mattel Game Studios, a global development organization dedicated to creating games based on its IP.
- The studio follows Mattel's February acquisition of 100% of Mattel163, previously a joint venture with NetEase.
- The studio employs nearly 300 staff across Los Angeles and Hangzhou.
- Mattel Game Studios will oversee existing mobile games, upcoming free-to-play titles, and releases on platforms like Roblox and Fortnite.
- The existing portfolio, including UNO!, UNO Wonder, Phase 10, and Skip-Bo, has over 550 million downloads and approximately 20 million monthly active players.
- The first title from Mattel Game Studios, Skeletor: Until Next Time!, released in June.
- Upcoming titles include UNO Wild, in soft launch, and an unnamed Hot Wheels title.
