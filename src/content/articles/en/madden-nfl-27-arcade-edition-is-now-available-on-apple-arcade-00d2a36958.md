- Madden NFL 27 Arcade Edition is now available on Apple Arcade.
- It includes no ads, in-app purchases, currencies, or paywalls.
- Apple Arcade costs $6.99 per month; new subscribers get a one-month free trial, and buyers of a new Apple device get three months free.
- The game features Quick Play, Franchise Mode, and other modes.
- It fully supports controllers on iPhone, iPad, Mac, and Apple TV.
- It offers the same 11-on-11 gameplay as Madden NFL Mobile.
