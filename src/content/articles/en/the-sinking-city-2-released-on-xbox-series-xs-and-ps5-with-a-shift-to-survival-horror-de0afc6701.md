- The Sinking City 2 was released for Xbox Series X|S and PlayStation 5 on 2026-08-18.
- It shifts from the original's open-world detective format to a semi-open-world survival horror experience, emphasizing combat, exploration, and survival.
- New enemy types include Slither Worms and Abominations, Stygian Harvesters, Acheronian Juggernauts, Lethian Revenants, and Deep One Raiders.
- The game features a new standalone story following Calvin and Faye, not requiring knowledge of the original game.
