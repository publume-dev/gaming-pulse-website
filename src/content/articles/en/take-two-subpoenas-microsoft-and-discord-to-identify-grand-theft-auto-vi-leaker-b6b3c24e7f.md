- Take-Two Interactive has subpoenaed Microsoft and Discord to identify the leaker 'Cyberleek' who leaked footage from an older build of Grand Theft Auto VI.
- Both companies must comply with the requests by September 4.
- The subpoena to Microsoft requests all internal business and investigative records related to the persona 'cyberleek' and references a GitHub repository where leaked content was posted.
- Discord has been asked to provide records of specific users and servers, including one affiliated with content creator Matthew Judge.
- Take-Two currently has no information regarding Cyberleek's identity, according to Bloomberg News.
- The GTA VI leaker has also posted threats targeting other large game publishers if they do not follow a manifesto concerning digital pre-orders, day-one DLC, and online game preservation.
