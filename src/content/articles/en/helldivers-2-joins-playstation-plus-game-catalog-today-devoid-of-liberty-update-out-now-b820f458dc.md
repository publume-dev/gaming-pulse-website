- Helldivers 2 is now available in the PlayStation Plus Game Catalog for Extra and Premium members as of August 12, 2026.
- The Devoid of Liberty update (version 7.0.0) is released today, adding new Illuminate enemies (Wretch and Crusher), a new Void biome, a level cap increase to 300, SEAF NPC allies, and various gameplay improvements.
- The Helldivers 2 x Warhammer 40,000 crossover, titled Castellan's Creed Legendary Warbond, launches today alongside the update.
