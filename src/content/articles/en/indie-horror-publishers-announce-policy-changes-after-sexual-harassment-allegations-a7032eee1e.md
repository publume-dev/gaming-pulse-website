- In a Game Developer report, former Critical Reflex community manager Evelina Zemljic published a YouTube video on December 14, 2025, accusing Critical Reflex CEO Rita Lebedeva and former co-worker Ted Hentschke, now CEO and co-founder of Black Lantern Collective, of mistreatment.
- Former Dread XP colleagues Cleveland Mosher and Star independently alleged that Hentschke sent inappropriate messages and shared graphic pornography in the workplace; Hentschke denied the claims, saying any such content would have come from games being reviewed for publishing.
- Two developers pitching games to Critical Reflex, Emily Pitcher and Chantal Ryan, told Game Developer that Hentschke made sexist or sexualized comments; Hentschke confirmed one conversation about wishlists and denied sexual harassment.
- Critical Reflex acknowledged delayed employment contracts but denied systemic payroll problems, and denied that Lebedeva harassed Zemljic.
- Black Lantern Collective established an anonymous whistleblowing form reviewed by its COO; Critical Reflex said it added a dedicated HR director role, formal communication platforms, an Employee Assistance Program, and expanded whistleblowing pathways.
- The harassment accusations are disputed by the accused parties and are reported as allegations from named and anonymous sources, not adjudicated facts.

Accusers told Game Developer they will not benefit from the companies' announced changes, and as of eight months after the initial allegations, none of the companies involved had released a public statement about the accusations. The article does not establish whether any formal investigation found wrongdoing or whether Hentschke or Lebedeva faced internal consequences.
