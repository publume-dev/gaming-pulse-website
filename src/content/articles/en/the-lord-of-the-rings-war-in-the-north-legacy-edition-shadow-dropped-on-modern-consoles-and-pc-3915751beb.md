- The Lord of the Rings: War in the North Legacy Edition is now available on Xbox Series X|S, PS5, Nintendo Switch, Switch 2, and PC.
- It is priced at $17.99 until August 25, 2026, when it increases to $19.99.
- The remaster runs at 60fps and adds bug fixes, enemy lock-on, and the ability to hot-swap characters.

- Aspyr, which remastered the game, says it plans further Lord of the Rings re-releases in the future but has not specified which titles.
