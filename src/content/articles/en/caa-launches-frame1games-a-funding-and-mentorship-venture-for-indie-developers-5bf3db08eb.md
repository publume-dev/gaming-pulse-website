- Creative Artists Agency (CAA) has launched Frame1Games, a funding accelerator for independent game developers that provides multi-million-dollar capital, mentorship, and access to strategic partners.
- The venture is led by veteran indie game investor Jared Yeager, known from Indie Fund, and was spearheaded by CAA's head of games Derek Douglas.
- Frame1Games includes a curated marketplace to showcase selected games, an unusual feature for such programs.
