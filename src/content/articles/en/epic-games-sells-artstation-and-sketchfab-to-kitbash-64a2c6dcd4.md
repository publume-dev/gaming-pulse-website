- Epic Games has sold ArtStation and Sketchfab to KitBash for an undisclosed fee, just over five years after acquiring both platforms in 2021.
- Epic stated the divestiture is to prioritize building Unreal Engine 6, Fortnite, the Epic Games Store, and its wider developer ecosystem.
- KitBash co-CEO Banks Boutté said the company will strive to 'protect what users value most about each platform' and will not change pricing for ArtStation, Sketchfab, or KitBash products as part of the deal.
