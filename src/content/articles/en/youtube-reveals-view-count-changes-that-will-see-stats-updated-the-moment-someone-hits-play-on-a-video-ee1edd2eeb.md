- Starting August 24, 2026, YouTube will count a view the moment a video begins to play, with no minimum watch time, across all formats including VOD, Shorts, and Live.
- YouTube says this change will not impact creators' earnings or eligibility for the YouTube Partner Program.
- Original analytics and performance metrics will continue to exist within YouTube's Engaged views banner.
- Creators will likely see their total view counts increase faster moving forward after August 24, 2026.
