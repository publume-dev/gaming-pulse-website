- Infinity Ward's beta update for Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 buffed the ISO Nightshade SMG, PPSH, Krait P68, Han 86, and Hyeon Burst, and nerfed the M4 Hurricane variant.
- Infinity Ward stated that the time to kill in Modern Warfare 4 is "much closer to where we wanted it to be" after Weekend 1 feedback.
- Studio Multiplayer Creative Director Joe Cecot said Infinity Ward's communication has improved compared to Modern Warfare 2 (2022), with the team and leadership posting in unison.
