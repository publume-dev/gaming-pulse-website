- At least 20 soldiers from the 3rd Infantry Division at Fort Stewart have reenlisted after the U.S. Army offered time off for the GTA 6 launch.
- Eligible soldiers who reenlist for two to six years get four days off to play GTA 6, with sign-ups open until November 14, 2026.
- The flyer for the GTA 6 reenlistment deal was signed by commanding officer Lt. Col. Ryan D. Hodgson.
- About 130 soldiers are eligible for the reenlistment offer.
