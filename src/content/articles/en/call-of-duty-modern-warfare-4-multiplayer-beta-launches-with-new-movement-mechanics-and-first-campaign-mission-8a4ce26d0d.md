- The Early Access Beta is now available on PlayStation 5, with Weekend One running through August 25 at 10:00am PT, and Weekend Two (Open Beta) from August 28 at 10:00am PT through September 1 at 10:00am PT.
- New movement mechanics include pipe climbs, supine slides, and mantle slides, along with a Mobility Course for training.
- The beta includes six 6v6 maps: Rooftops, Silkworm, Transit 213, Cachette, Lotus, and Lithium, and modes including Team Deathmatch, Domination, Hardpoint, Kill Confirmed, Search and Destroy, plus new modes Inflation, Hijack, and Kill Block.
- For the first time in Call of Duty beta history, a Campaign mission titled 'Entrenched' is playable in the beta.
- Weekend Two adds Call of Duty: Warzone Resurgence on the new Zodiac map.
- Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 launches on October 23.
