- Valve has begun notifying recent Steam hardware customers of a personal data breach that may have exposed their name, address, phone number, and email.
- The potentially affected purchases are Steam Deck, Steam Machine, or Steam Controller made within Europe in the past 90 days.
- Passwords and payment details are not thought to be at risk.
- The breach involved CEVA Logistics, Valve's European distribution firm, which receives delivery-related information from Valve.
- Valve tells customers to expect fake email, SMS, or phone messages referencing their hardware order and appearing to come from Steam, Valve, or a delivery company, and to treat them as fake.
- The number of affected customers has not been disclosed.
