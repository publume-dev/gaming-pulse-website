- Saber Interactive denied replacing a writer with AI on Rideshare Stimulator, stating the story was written entirely by real people and that the staff of writers has tripled since 2023.
- Former lead writer Stella Sacco alleged she was replaced by ChatGPT midway through development and that passenger voices were AI-generated.
- The Steam page for Rideshare Stimulator currently has no AI disclosure despite Valve's requirement to flag generative AI content.
