- **Release date**: Star Wars: Smuggler's Gambit will be available in Fortnite on August 16, 2026.
- **Park tie-in**: The experience can link to a player's MyDisney account to unlock a special experience on Millennium Falcon: Smuggler's Run at Disney theme parks; details of that experience have not been disclosed.
- **Gameplay**: It features three player classes (Pilot, Gunner, Engineer) and multiple missions including "The Imperial Patrol" and "The Heist."
