- The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt – Remastered launches on September 29 as a free upgrade for all owners of The Witcher 3 on Xbox Series X|S.
- Players who own only the base game will receive both Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine expansions for free, including Xbox One players.
- The remaster includes reworked lighting, improved ray tracing, new finishers, adaptive camera, reimagined skill trees, transmogrification, cross-platform mod support, expanded photo mode, and Xbox Play Anywhere.
- The third expansion, Songs of the Past, will release in 2027 and introduce the new realm Letten, Dandelion's homeland, with new monsters, mutagens, and a chain weapon.
