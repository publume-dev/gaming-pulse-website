- CWA union members staged the protest outside Bethesda’s Rockville, Maryland office while Xbox CEO Asha Sharma visited, displaying a giant inflatable rat and red flags that the union said represented each person laid off under Sharma’s leadership.
- In the union’s account, CWA member Nathan Hahn said the protest was meant to force leadership to reckon with the human cost of repeated game-industry layoffs.
- Xbox had confirmed plans to cut 3,200 jobs across its game division by the end of the current fiscal year, starting with immediate cuts at Obsidian, id Software, and ZeniMax Online Studios; this was the fifth round of mass Xbox layoffs in three years.
- The protest account comes from a union statement to the outlet; the article did not include a response from Microsoft or Bethesda or an independent count of protesters or flags.

- CWA unions in the U.S. and Canada have taken legal action against Microsoft over the latest job cuts; CWA Canada president Carmel Smyth said workers were “unlawfully fired” without notice or union discussion.

- Further “Save Our Devs” rallies are scheduled for August 18, 2026, outside Xbox studios in Irvine, Austin, Dallas, Seattle, Albany, Rockville, Hunt Valley, Montreal, and Minneapolis.
