- Twitch has rolled out a new setting that by default allows channel content to train Amazon's generative AI content models. 
- The setting can be disabled by navigating to the Security and Privacy tab of Twitch user settings and unchecking the 'Training for Generative AI' toggle.
- Disabling the setting does not opt users out of Amazon using channel content for other uses, per the Twitch Privacy Notice.
- The top post on Twitch's User Voice forums, with more than 13,000 votes and 4,000 comments, requests making all AI features optional and opted out by default.
