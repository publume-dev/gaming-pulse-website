- Panic is refunding tariff surcharges charged on Playdate orders during a specific window in 2026, after receiving its own refund from the US government following a Supreme Court ruling that the tariffs were unlawful.
- Co-founder Cabel Sasser confirmed the company initially absorbed tariffs, then added a clearly labeled tariff line item (confirmed at 19%) during the affected period.
- Sasser stated, "It's just not our money to keep," explaining the decision to refund customers.
