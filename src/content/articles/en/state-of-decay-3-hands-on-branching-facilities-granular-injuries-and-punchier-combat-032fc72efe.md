- Facilities have branching upgrade paths that specialize in specific functions; character traits can boost effectiveness, such as medical traits increasing recovery rate.
- Procedurally generated characters have unique perks and quirks that evolve, for example a survivor with 'takes long showers' uses more water but excels in melee.
- A granular injury system calculates damage to specific body parts and applies debuffs like reduced weapon accuracy and movement speed.
- Infestation networks spread across the open world and into adjacent towns; destroying each infestation requires finding four weak points, which triggers a larger zombie horde.
- Combat feels smoother and punchier, with a running slide to knock down zombies and melee finishers, plus a greater emphasis on third-person shooting.
- Drop-in/drop-out co-op is supported with no exploration limitations.
- A dynamic difficulty system adjusts challenge based on player performance, easing off if the player frequently fails.
- A closed playtest is planned for late 2026, with full release in 2027 on Xbox Series X|S, PlayStation 5, and PC via Steam.
