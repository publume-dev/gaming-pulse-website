- W4 Games raised $18 million in Series B funding led by Tencent, bringing total funding to $33 million.
- W4 Games signed an agreement with Tencent to support the development and adoption of W4 technology and bolster the Godot ecosystem in Asia.
- W4 Games plans to add ten to fifteen full-time employees in the next 12 months, focusing on Asia as part of expanding its international team.
- Godot Engine creator Juan Linietsky expressed excitement about working with Tencent to establish Godot as a leading platform for game development.
