Key points:
- Digital Schoolhouse and Roblox have partnered to offer free classroom tools for teaching Roblox game development, including interactive Roblox Studio lesson plans.
- The classroom version is a closed, teacher-supervised environment, with an offline version available for schools that lack reliable devices or internet access.
- The teacher toolkit includes safeguarding guidance, parent-facing materials, and curriculum alignment support.
- Open-source curriculum materials will be available for download on the Digital Schoolhouse website at a later date, aligned with national computing curriculum frameworks.
