- Pearl Abyss reported that Crimson Desert sold about 2.11 million copies in Q2 2026, bringing total sales to over 6 million units since its March 2026 launch.
- The Switch 2 version has reached a playable baseline, with Pearl Abyss targeting a release in the first half of 2027.
- Pearl Abyss is conducting extensive research on multiplayer modes for Crimson Desert, though it has not confirmed whether they will be added.
- The first Crimson Desert DLC is in development for release this year, with an announcement planned during Q3 2026.
- Pearl Abyss stated that a mobile version of Crimson Desert is not planned.
