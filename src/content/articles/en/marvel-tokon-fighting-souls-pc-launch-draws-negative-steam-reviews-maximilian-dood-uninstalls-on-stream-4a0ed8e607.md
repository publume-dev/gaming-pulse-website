- Marvel Tokon: Fighting Souls launched for PC and PlayStation 5 on August 6, 2026.
- The PC version's Steam rating fell to 44% positive in all languages at launch, then to 41% positive overall and a "Mostly Negative" 39% positive in English.
- Maximilian Dood uninstalled the PC version during an August 7 stream, saying the port was "really s**t" and that "It's making me like the game significantly less."
- Maximilian Dood said he will exclusively play the PlayStation 5 version because the PC version is too frustrating, and there is no cross-progression between PC and PS5.
- According to Maximilian Dood, the PS5 version is smooth even with high ping (100+ ms), unlike the PC version.
