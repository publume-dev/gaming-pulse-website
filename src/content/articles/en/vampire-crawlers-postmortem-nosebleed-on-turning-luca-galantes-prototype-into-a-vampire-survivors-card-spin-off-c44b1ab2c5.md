- Nosebleed Interactive collaborated with Poncle on Vampire Crawlers, a card-game spin-off of Vampire Survivors, starting from an early prototype by Vampire Survivors creator Luca Galante.
- Early in development, Nosebleed used placeholder content and then paused other work to align Vampire Crawlers' names and stats with the Vampire Survivors IP before Poncle approved the next build.
- A late-development lunchtime meeting with Luca Galante led Nosebleed to add the Gems system, which the studio says improved the game.
