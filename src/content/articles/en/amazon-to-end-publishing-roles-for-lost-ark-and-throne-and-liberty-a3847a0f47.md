- Amazon will end its publishing role for Lost Ark, with service operations transitioning to developer Smilegate by early 2027.
- Amazon will transition publishing of Throne and Liberty to developer NC (formerly NCSoft) by the end of Q4 2026, with NC working with FirstSpark Games on a unified global service.
