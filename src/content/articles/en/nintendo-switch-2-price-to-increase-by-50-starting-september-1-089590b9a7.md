- Nintendo will raise the price of the Switch 2 console from $450 to $499.99, effective September 1, 2026.
- After the price hike, two new Switch 2 bundles will be released: one including Mario Kart World for $549.99 in late September, and one including Nintendo Switch Sports Resort for $529.99 on October 22, 2026.
