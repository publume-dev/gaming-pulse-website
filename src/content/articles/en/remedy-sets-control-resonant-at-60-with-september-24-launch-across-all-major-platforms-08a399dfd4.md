- Remedy CEO Jean-Charles Gaudechon said Control Resonant will be priced at $60, which he described as attractive for an AAA title, and will be available across all major platforms from day one.
- Control Resonant is scheduled to launch on September 24.
- Remedy says Control Resonant has more than 1.5 million wishlists across all platforms, and pre-orders ranked among the top three PlayStation games in the US, Germany, and Brazil.
- Remedy reported Q2 2026 revenue of €10.2 million, down 40% year over year, and an operating loss of €3.9 million, compared with a €0.5 million loss in the same quarter a year earlier.
- Remedy reported first-half 2026 revenue fell 23.1% to €23.3 million, with an operating loss of €2.9 million.
- Remedy said FBC: Firebreak, which underperformed commercially, will no longer receive major content updates.
