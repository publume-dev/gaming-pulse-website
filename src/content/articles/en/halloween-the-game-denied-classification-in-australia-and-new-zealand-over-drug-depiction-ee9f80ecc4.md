- Australia's Classification Board refused classification for IllFonic's Halloween: The Game, blocking its sale in Australia and New Zealand.
- IllFonic said the refusal was based on the presence of a controlled substance, not on Michael Myers or the game's kills.
- IllFonic confirmed the game cannot be sold physically or digitally in Australia and New Zealand.
- The specific controlled substance is not confirmed; reports suggest cannabis provides a gameplay buff to Michael Myers, but IllFonic has not confirmed that detail.
- It is not clear whether IllFonic will modify the game for Australia and New Zealand, or whether the refusal affects the September launch in other regions.
