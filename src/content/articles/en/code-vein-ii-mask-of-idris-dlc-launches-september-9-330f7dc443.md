- **Launch date**: Code Vein II: Mask of Idris DLC launches on September 9, 2026.
- **New content**: The DLC introduces a new story with two new partners: Liv Voda and Lou Magmell.
- **New weapons**: The DLC adds new weapons and Formae, including the Grave Knocker and Sword of Karma.
- **Free update**: A free update for all players launches alongside the DLC, adding new features and enhancements.
