- First-quarter profit rose 175.5% year-on-year to ¥13.2 billion ($82.9 million); the same report separately lists the growth as 175.7%.
- Square Enix attributed the increase to the Digital Entertainment segment, especially strong HD Games sales.
- HD Games net sales climbed 98.8% to ¥17.7 billion ($111.1 million), and HD Games operating income rose 510% to ¥6.1 billion ($38.2 million). The article's introduction mislabels that operating income figure as sales before the detailed section clarifies it.
- The gains were driven by Final Fantasy VII Rebirth launching on Nintendo Switch 2, Xbox Series X|S, and Microsoft Store.
- Total units sold in the quarter were 7.38 million, up from 4.01 million a year earlier; it is unclear whether this covers HD Games only or all segments.
- Square Enix maintained its consolidated full-year forecast for the fiscal year announced on May 14, 2026.
