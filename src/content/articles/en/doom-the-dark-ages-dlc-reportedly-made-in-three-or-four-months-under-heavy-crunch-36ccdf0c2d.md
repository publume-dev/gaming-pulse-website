- Former id Software producer Andrew Willis said the majority of Doom: The Dark Ages – Revelations was made in three or four months under heavy crunch.
- Per Willis, two months involved at least 12-hour days, with some days reaching 17 hours.
- Willis described a "blurry line" with no official company mandate, but felt it necessary to finish the project at player-expected quality.
- He added that some team members with 20 years in Hollywood and 10 in game development called it the worst crunch they'd been part of.
