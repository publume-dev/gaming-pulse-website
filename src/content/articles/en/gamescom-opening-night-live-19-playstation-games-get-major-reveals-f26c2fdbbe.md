- Ananta launches on PS5 on January 15, 2027, featuring a new city inspired by Shanghai and Hangzhou, hidden martial arts masters, and new vehicles including flying cars and hang gliders.
- Exodus launches on PS5 on April 7, 2027, confirmed by its official release date trailer at Gamescom Opening Night Live.
- Final Fantasy VII Revelation launches on PS5 in Spring 2027, with a new trailer revealing the Weapons and the Highwind.
- Path of Exile 2 Version 1.0 launches on PS5 on December 11, bringing major new content across campaign, endgame, character progression, skills, and loot.
- Rainbow Six Tactics, a new single-player turn-based game in the Tom Clancy universe, is coming in 2027.
- Resonance: A Plague Tale Legacy launches on PS5 on August 27, with its launch trailer shown at Gamescom.
- Silent Hill: Townfall releases on PS5 on September 24, with new footage revealing its first-person perspective and English voice cast including Jovan Adepo, Terry O'Quinn, and Kezia Burrows.
- Mewgenics launches on PS5 on September 8.
- Turok: Origins launches on PS5 in Spring 2027, with a new story trailer shown at Gamescom.
- The enhanced Crimson Desert update is available now as a free update for all players with the base game.
- Warlock: Dungeons & Dragons debuted extended gameplay at Gamescom and is coming in 2027.
