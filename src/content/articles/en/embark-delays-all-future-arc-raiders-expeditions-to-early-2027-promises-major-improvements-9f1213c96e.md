- Embark Studios delays all future Arc Raiders Expeditions until early 2027 to implement major improvements based on community feedback.
- Expedition 5 will run from September 8 to September 29, 2026, and will be the last until early 2027.
- Weekly Trials will pause for two weeks from September 22, 2026, until October 8, 2026.
- Players who completed all Expeditions will receive the Zenith outfit, and partially completed Caravans will become Ermal Vouchers of equal value.
- Consecutive buffs will persist during the pause between Expeditions.

Embark confirmed in May 2026 it would commit to two major updates per year, with the next, Frozen Trail, due in October 2026.
