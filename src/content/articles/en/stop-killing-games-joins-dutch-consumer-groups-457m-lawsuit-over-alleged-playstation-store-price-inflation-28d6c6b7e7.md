- Stop Killing Games said it is joining the $457 million lawsuit filed by Dutch consumer organization Stichting Massaschade & Consument against Sony over alleged PlayStation Store price inflation in the Netherlands.
- The group made the announcement in a YouTube video uploaded on August 10, 2026.
- It is demanding that Sony repay money allegedly earned from Dutch players through digital game purchases on the PlayStation Store.
- The movement described increased digital game prices as an unlawful “Sony Tax” and called for real alternatives to the PlayStation Store.
- Sony plans to stop printing physical games starting January 2028, a decision that has prompted multiple legal actions.
