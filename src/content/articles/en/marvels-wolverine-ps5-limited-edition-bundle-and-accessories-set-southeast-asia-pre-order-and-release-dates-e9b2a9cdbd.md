- PlayStation Southeast Asia’s announcement describes the PS5 Digital Edition – Marvel’s Wolverine Battle Yellow Limited Edition Bundle as including a PS5 Digital Edition, a matching DualSense wireless controller, and a game code for Marvel’s Wolverine, with the bundle available globally.
- Pre-orders for the bundle and accessories open August 19 at 10 AM local time, and the bundle and accessories are available from September 15 in limited quantities; Southeast Asian bundle pricing is SGD 849 / MYR 2,799 / IDR 11,399,000 / THB 20,990.
- The announcement also adds PS5 and PS5 Pro Battle Yellow console covers, PS5 Pro Adamantium console covers, and an Adamantium DualSense controller, sold in limited quantities through local retailers; in Singapore the Adamantium controller is exclusive to select retailer channels.
