- Alinea Analytics estimates Halo: Campaign Evolved sold approximately 1.2 million copies across Xbox, PS5, and Steam in its first two weeks, with a platform split of 42% Xbox, 41% PS5, and 17% Steam, and revenue around $67 million.
- Alinea's Rhys Elliott calls the sales figures 'soft,' citing the high price for a package lacking competitive multiplayer and offering less content than Halo: The Master Chief Collection.
