- Restart has laid off its entire editorial team, confirmed by news editor Imran Khan and editor-in-chief Brandy Berthelson.
- Restart launched in December 2024 with backing from marketing firm Moonrock and Walmart, and presented itself as independently operated despite that support.
- The report does not specify how many editorial staff were affected or whether Restart will continue without an editorial team.
