- Supermassive Games has begun a redundancy consultation covering up to 75 positions, its third round of job losses in three years.
- The studio previously cut about 90 staff in February 2024 and 35 more in July 2025, leaving headcount roughly half of its 2023 level.
- Its latest release, Directive 8020, shipped in May and currently has a mixed user rating on Steam from about 1,400 reviews.
