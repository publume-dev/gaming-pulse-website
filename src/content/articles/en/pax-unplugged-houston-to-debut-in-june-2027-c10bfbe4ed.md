- PAX Unplugged will debut at the George R. Brown Convention Center in Houston, Texas, from June 25 to June 27, 2027.
- The event will feature an expo floor for analogue gaming exhibitors, panels, tournaments, community meetups, and more.
- PAX event manager Sean Gonzalez stated the event is a chance to bring PAX back to PAX South fans. Tickets and media applications will be available later this year.
