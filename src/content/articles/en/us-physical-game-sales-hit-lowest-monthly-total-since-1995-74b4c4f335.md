- US physical software sales fell to $85 million in July 2026, the lowest monthly total since Circana began tracking in 1995.
- Hardware spending declined 29% year-over-year to $282 million in July 2026, the lowest July total since 2020.
- Total spending on content, hardware, and accessories fell 10% year-over-year to $4.5 billion in July 2026.
- Call of Duty: Black Ops 2 led the July 2026 US sales charts, driven by the launch of its PS4 and PS5 port.
- Switch 2 led July 2026 in hardware units sold, while PS5 led in revenue.

The data comes as Circana's analyst mentions a planned Switch 2 price increase on September 1, which could further affect hardware sales in coming months.
