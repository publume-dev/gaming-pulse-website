- Frontier Developments has signed a deal with Disney to develop an upcoming creative management simulation game.
- Frontier CEO Jo Cooke said the collaboration reinforces the company's strategy of focusing on creative management simulation franchises and aims to connect its community's creativity with a beloved brand.
- The announcement did not specify the game's title, release window, platforms, or scope.
- Frontier also reaffirmed that another unannounced Planet sim game is in development, while Planet Zoo 2 launches in October.
