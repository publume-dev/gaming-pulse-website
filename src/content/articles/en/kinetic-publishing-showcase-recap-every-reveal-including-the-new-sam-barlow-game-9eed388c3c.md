- Kinetic Publishing held its first showcase on August 12, 2026, revealing five titles for 2027-2028.
- PRECOGNITION, the new game from Sam Barlow (Her Story, Immortality) in collaboration with director Brandon Cronenberg, was revealed for Xbox Series X|S and PC with a Spring 2028 release window.
- Contact Protocol, a sci-fi horror game about starship paperwork, is launching for Xbox Series X|S and PC in Spring 2027.
- Crescent County, a witch-tech open world game by Electric Saint, is launching for Xbox Series X|S and PC in Summer 2027.
- Shellbound, a parasitic metroidvania from Fake Lobster, is launching for Xbox Series X|S and PC in Summer 2028.
- Aether Wizard Life, an action-RPG with farming and town-building from Vonder Games, is launching for Xbox Series X|S and PC in Autumn 2028.
