- Double Fine Productions has regained ownership of its intellectual property and publishing rights after separating from Xbox, as confirmed by CEO Tim Schafer during a podcast.
- The studio announced Amnesia Fortnight 2026 – Indie Reboot, a Kickstarter campaign launching August 31.
- In the campaign, backers will choose which prototypes to develop into small games, with a documentary by 2 Player Productions.
