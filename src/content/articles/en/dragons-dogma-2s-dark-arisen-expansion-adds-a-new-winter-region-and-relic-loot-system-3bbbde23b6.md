- The expansion adds a new region, Norgan, recommended for characters around level 40 and accessible early in the campaign.
- Introduces a relic loot system where mystery items are appraised by Jiera for rift crystals, encouraging exploration of Norgan.
- Includes 12 new Lost Rites dungeons with rare equipment, and players can travel between Norgan and the original map.
- Icy conditions introduce environmental challenges like freezing, avalanches, and wind that affect traversal and combat.

Director Kenta Kinoshita and producer Naoto Oyama confirm the relic system was inspired by the original Dark Arisen's cursed loot and aims to expand the open-world design.
