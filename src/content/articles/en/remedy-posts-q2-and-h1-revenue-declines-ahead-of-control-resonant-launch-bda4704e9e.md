- Remedy's Q2 2026 revenue was €10.2 million, down 40% year-on-year, with an operating loss of €3.9 million.
- H1 2026 revenue was €23.3 million, down 23.1% year-on-year, with an operating loss of €2.9 million.
- Remedy attributed the revenue declines to the prior launch of FBC: Firebreak and increased marketing for Control Resonant.
- Pre-orders for Control Resonant opened with healthy momentum and ranked among the top three pre-ordered PlayStation games in the US, Germany, and Brazil.
- Alan Wake 2 exceeded three million lifetime sales.
- Control Resonant is scheduled to launch on September 24.
