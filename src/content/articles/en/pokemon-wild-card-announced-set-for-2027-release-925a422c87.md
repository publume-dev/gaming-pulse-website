- **Announcement**: The animated movie Pokémon: Wild Card was announced during the closing ceremony of the 2026 Pokémon World Championships.
- **Release window**: It is set to release starting in 2027, marking the first new Pokémon animated movie in seven years.
- **Protagonist and focus**: The movie's protagonist is a Pokémon TCG player, and the teaser highlighted Mimikyu and the Trading Card Game.
- **Production team**: Animation will be done by CloverWorks, directed by Katsuhiko Kitada, produced by Story inc., with character designs by Akemi Hayashi.
