- UK PS5 sales rose 33% and Xbox Series X|S sales rose 34% following the GTA 6 Netflix preview, per Nielsen IQ data reported by GamesIndustry.biz.
- PS5 Pro consoles are selling out nationwide in the UK, with retailers Argos and Smyths Toys only offering base PS5 models.
- The extended GTA 6 preview was Netflix's most-viewed title last week, reaching 31.1 million views in four days.
- UK hardware sales are down 14.4% year-on-year, with PS5 sales down 32% and Xbox Series X|S sales up 3.1%.
- The GTA 6 preview received 22 million views on YouTube in one week.
- Sensor Tower data shows Netflix's US mobile viewership increased by 35% hour-over-hour during the preview premiere.

The report attributes hardware sales increases to the GTA 6 preview but does not provide causal evidence or control for other factors (e.g., Call of Duty marketing, seasonal trends).
