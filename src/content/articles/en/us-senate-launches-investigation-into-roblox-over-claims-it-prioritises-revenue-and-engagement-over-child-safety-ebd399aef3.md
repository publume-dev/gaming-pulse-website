- The US Senate Judiciary Subcommittee on Crime and Counterterrorism has launched an investigation into Roblox, claiming the platform 'appears to prioritise revenue and engagement metrics' over child safety.
- Senators Josh Hawley and Dick Durbin sent a letter to Roblox CEO David Baszucki requesting documents on child safety, abuse, bullying, and financial exploitation, with a deadline of August 31, 2026.
- Roblox reported over 65,000 suspected child exploitation cases to the National Centre for Missing & Exploited Children in 2025, more than double the number in 2024.

Roblox chief safety officer Matt Kaufman stated the platform is 'built on the foundation of safety' and looks forward to sharing facts about child protection efforts.
